# FSMI-Open
Florida Seafloor Mapping Initiative

Name: Florida Seafloor Mapping Initiative Regional Digital Elevation Models (DEMs)

Description: Digital elevation models derived from LiDAR and Sonar collections from 0 to 200 meters of depth off the coast of the state of Florida between 2023 and 2026.

Documentation: https://github.com/Florida-Seafloor-Mapping-Initiative/FSMI-Open

ManagedBy: https://www.floridagio.gov/
